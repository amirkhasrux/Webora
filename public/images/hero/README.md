# Hero Slider Images

## Current Setup

The hero slider currently uses beautiful gradient placeholders with text overlays. These are perfect for demo purposes and look professional right out of the box.

## How to Add Your Own Images

When you're ready to use your own images, follow these steps:

### 1. Add Your Images
Place your hero slider images in this folder (`public/images/hero/`):
- **slide1.jpg** - Your main hero image
- **slide2.jpg** - Second slider image
- **slide3.jpg** - Third slider image

### 2. Image Specifications
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Recommended Size:** 1920x1080px (Full HD) or larger
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 for best results
- **File Size:** Optimize to under 500KB per image for fast loading
- **Content:** High-quality, professional images that represent your business
